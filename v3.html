About

Becky Sikasa is a Glasgow-based, German singer-songwriter, producer and multi-instrumentalist. Her music brings together Kenyan, Swiss and German roots and a Glasgow home: rich, introspective songs that sit between neo-soul, alternative R&B and indie-pop.

Both of her EPs — Twelve Wooden Boxes and The Writings and the Pictures and the Song — were shortlisted for the Scottish Album of the Year Award. She was named BBC Introducing Scottish Artist of the Year 2025, has toured with Nile Rodgers, and has shared stages with Sam Smith, Joy Crookes, corto.alto and The Crane Wives.

Annie Lennox personally invited her to play the main stage at Wilderness Festival, and has championed her ever since.
