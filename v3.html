Sky Is Close

Written on my upright piano, mostly recorded in Cologne and finished in Edinburgh at the end of summer. To live in fear is not an option.Becky
Stream YouTube

Becky Sikasa is a Glasgow-based, German singer-songwriter, producer and multi-instrumentalist. Her music brings together Kenyan, Swiss and German roots and a Glasgow home: rich, introspective songs that sit between neo-soul, alternative R&B and indie-pop.

Both of her EPs were shortlisted for the Scottish Album of the Year Award. She was named BBC Introducing Scottish Artist of the Year 2025, has toured with Nile Rodgers, and has shared stages with Sam Smith, Joy Crookes, corto.alto and The Crane Wives.

“She’s phenomenal. With a voice like that, with a songwriting capacity like that, this is a real artist.”Annie Lennox
