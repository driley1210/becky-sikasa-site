About Glasgow, via Cologne

Becky Sikasa is a Glasgow-based, German singer-songwriter, producer and multi-instrumentalist. Her music brings together Kenyan, Swiss and German roots and a Glasgow home: rich, introspective songs that sit between neo-soul, alternative R&B and indie-pop.

Both of her EPs were shortlisted for the Scottish Album of the Year Award. She was named BBC Introducing Scottish Artist of the Year 2025, has toured with Nile Rodgers, and has shared stages with Sam Smith, Joy Crookes, corto.alto and The Crane Wives. BBC Introducing in Scotland has played every track she has released since 2022, with regular support from BBC Radio 1 and 1Xtra.

Annie Lennox personally invited her to play the main stage at Wilderness Festival, and has championed her ever since.

“She’s phenomenal. With a voice like that, with a songwriting capacity like that, this is a real artist.”Annie Lennox
