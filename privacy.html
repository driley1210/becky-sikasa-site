Last updated 8 November 2026

This site belongs to Becky Sikasa, who is the data controller for any personal data collected through it. If you have any question about your data, email becky@beckysikasa.com.

What we collect, and why

If you join the mailing list. The sign-up form on this site is provided by Openstage. The details you enter (your email address and anything else the form asks for) go to Openstage, who hold them for us so we can send you news about Becky’s music, shows and merchandise. We do this with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email, or by emailing us. Openstage’s own privacy notice is at openstage.live/privacy.

If you accept cookies. With your permission we use advertising and measurement tools from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok and Google. They set cookies and collect information such as your IP address, device and browser details, the pages you look at here and an identifier for you on their platform. We use this to see how people find the site, to measure whether advertising for Becky’s releases is working, and to show her music to people who are likely to be interested. None of this happens unless you choose Accept on the cookie notice.

Everyone. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, pages requested) for security and operation. Links to Spotify, YouTube, See Tickets, Instagram and TikTok take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Where your data goes

Meta, TikTok, Google, Openstage and GitHub may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they rely on the UK’s data-transfer safeguards (such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

How long we keep it

Mailing-list details are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Advertising cookies expire after the periods set by each provider (typically 90 days for Meta and TikTok, up to two years for Google). Our own consent cookie lasts until you clear it.

Your rights

Under UK data-protection law you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email becky@beckysikasa.com and we will respond within a month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be flagged on the site.
